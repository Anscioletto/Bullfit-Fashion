# Bullfit Fashion: Shopify E-commerce for English & French Bulldogs

A Shopify store for **Bullfit Fashion**, an Italian clothing and accessories brand from Cava de' Tirreni, made only for English and French Bulldogs. It sells clothing cut to the bulldog's body, the patented **B-Cool** thermoregulating line that helps prevent heatstroke, beds made in Italy, skin care for sensitive skin, and matching items for owners.

🔗 **Live store:** [bullfitfashion.com](https://bullfitfashion.com)

---

## 📌 Overview

Designer **Emi Nava** founded Bullfit Fashion after more than twenty years in international luxury fashion. The brand calls itself the first clothing line designed for English and French Bulldogs, and has been *"Bulldog specialists since 2018"*. Its tagline is *"Your bulldog doesn't sweat. We do, for him."*

The store has two jobs:

* **Dress bulldogs properly**: every size is modeled on bulldog anatomy, not on generic dog patterns. The clothes also fit pugs, Boston Terriers, American Bulldogs and Boxers.
* **Protect brachycephalic breeds**: bulldogs don't sweat and overheat easily, so the B-Cool line helps prevent heatstroke. The brand works with specialized veterinary clinics across Italy.

## ❄️ Signature Line: B-Cool

B-Cool is the brand's main innovation: a **thermoregulating line** for bulldogs and other brachycephalic breeds. It is built around a **patented membrane** that switches on when the dog's body temperature rises. It works on its own, **without wetting the coat** and **without thermal shock**.

It comes in **4 formats and 7 bulldog sizes**:

| Product | Function |
|---|---|
| **B-COOL T-SHIRT** | Cooling shirt for walks and hot days |
| **B-COOL NECK COOLER** | Cools the neck area |
| **B-COOL UNDERWEAR** | Cooling dog underwear |
| **B-COOLCCIA** | Thermoregulating bed with a fully removable cover (50% cotton, 50% polyester) |

* **Tested every day** on the brand's own bulldogs.
* **Used by Italian veterinary clinics** for brachycephalic dogs waking up from anesthesia.
* **Prevention, not emergency care**: the FAQ says what to do if a dog already has heatstroke.

## 📏 Bulldog Fit

* **Bulldog-specific sizing**, which also fits pugs, Boston Terriers, American Bulldogs and Boxers.
* **Size calculator**: weight comes first, then chest and length to confirm.
* **Size guide**: measure the chest at its widest point, just behind the front legs, with the dog standing.
* **B-Cool tip**: the fabric is stretchy and fits close, so if a dog is between two sizes, choose the smaller one.
